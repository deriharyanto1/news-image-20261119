# The World Today — 19 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of
Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn entirely in code.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) draws the page
with a hand-made vector stroke font and a hand-written PNG encoder, and
writes the finished 1024x1024 image to `/output/entry.png`.

Top story: the first three-way summit of Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and
Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen at APEC China 2026. Also inside: Anthropic's
record $81.3 billion IPO, the GTA 6 global rollout, Israel's ground
operation in Gaza, another attack on the Kerch Bridge, Hakeem Jeffries
set to become the first African-American Speaker of the US House, and
briefs on North Korea's missile launches, Voyager 1 reaching one
light-day from Earth, the EU's 45 billion euro loan to Ukraine, the
Houthis on the Red Sea coast, and Estonia closing its border with Russia.

Run it twice and the two images are byte-identical.

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119)
